#E-Klass #1/72 #2025 #2026 #WIP

[WIP] KrAZ ATZ-22, 1/72, E-Klass

I was so happy when I found this old model in the exchange stash at the IPMS Ireland Nationals 2025. It is simple, and it’s a great subject - an old civilian truck, which not only did I see in person many times, but also grew to love in Snowrunner (a video game). KrAZ-258 It is an old soviet 6x6 truck, manufactured at KrAZ plant in Ukraine. Younger people may know it as Tayga 6455B in Snowrunner.

#WoodenKit #1/72 #WAK #PaperKit #2025 #2026

Galway Hooker, 1/72, WAK

Picked up this paper model from WAK at the IPMS Ireland Nationals 2025. I wanted to build a Galway Hooker for many years now, but there was no wooden kit available. I didn’t consider paper models at all, having no experience with them. Thankfully there was a completed Galway Hooker model on display in the show, so I got to see it. I was so impressed with it, that my wife instantly bought the kit for me.
